1.Bench press / floor press / weighted push-ups

3 sets × 8–15 reps

Superlift page ↗ · Floor press page ↗

✓Posture: Keep shoulder blades stable, feet planted for bench press, wrists stacked over elbows, and elbows roughly 30–45° from your torso. For push-ups, keep a straight line from head to heels.

✕Avoid: Flaring elbows, bouncing at the bottom, sagging hips, or letting wrists fold backward.

2.Pullover

3 sets × 8–15 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Keep a slight elbow bend throughout. Brace your abs and keep ribs down as the dumbbell moves behind your head.

✕Avoid: Overarching your lower back, bending the elbows more as you lower, or forcing an excessive shoulder stretch.

3.Overhead press

3 sets × 8–15 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Brace abs and glutes, keep ribs down, wrists over elbows, and press smoothly overhead.

✕Avoid: Leaning backward, shrugging excessively, or using leg drive when doing a strict press.

4.Biceps curl

3 sets × 12–20 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Stand tall, keep elbows close to your sides, wrists straight, and lower the weights slowly.

✕Avoid: Swinging your torso, drifting elbows forward, or bending the wrists.

5.Skullcrusher / overhead extension

3 sets × 15–20 reps

Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Keep upper arms steady and bend mainly at the elbows. Maintain straight, neutral wrists and control the lowering phase.

✕Avoid: Flaring elbows, letting upper arms swing, or using a load that causes elbow discomfort.

6.Rear delt fly

3 sets × 15–20 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Hinge at the hips with a neutral back, soften the elbows, and move arms outward under control.

✕Avoid: Using momentum, shrugging, or turning the movement into a row.

7.Lateral raise

3 sets × 15–20 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Keep a small bend in your elbows and raise arms out to around shoulder height with control.

✕Avoid: Swinging the dumbbells, shrugging, or lifting much higher than shoulder level if uncomfortable.

8.Front squat

3 sets × 8–15 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Keep torso tall, brace your trunk, maintain full-foot pressure, and let knees track in line with toes.

✕Avoid: Heels lifting, knees collapsing inward, or rounding your back under load.

9.Romanian deadlift

3 sets × 8–15 reps

Tutorial video ↗ · Superlift page ↗

✓Posture: Push hips backward, keep knees softly bent and spine neutral, and keep the weights close to your legs.

✕Avoid: Squatting the movement, rounding your back, or lowering beyond the point where you can maintain a neutral spine.
